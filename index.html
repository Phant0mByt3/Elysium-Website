Ages Long Past

The History of Elysium

Four ages shaped the world you will walk through. Each left its scars, its wonders, and its warnings.

Age One

The Ancient Age

The first civilizations rise, raising cities of stone and forging the earliest pacts between mortals and the old magics of the world.

Age Two

The Sundering

A catastrophic event changes the world forever, tearing open the lands that would one day become Vethmoor and scattering ancient power across the continents.

Age Three

The Age of Darkness

Kingdoms fall and monsters spread across the ruins, forcing survivors into isolated strongholds as the old order collapses.

Age Four

The Age of Reclamation

Heroes return to rebuild civilization, founding the factions and kingdoms that players will encounter across Elysium today.

Reputation System

Actions across the world raise or lower your standing with each faction, from Hostile to Exalted. Higher standing unlocks unique quests, gear, and dialogue.

Alliances & Rivalries

Factions are interconnected. Aiding Silverwatch may raise suspicion with the Ashen Circle, while standing with the Dawnbound Concord can open doors — or close them — with the Duskward Pact.

Vision
